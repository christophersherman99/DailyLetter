Method & privacy
  • Pipeline. A Python job on GitHub Actions runs every weekday morning: it pulls prices, fundamentals and news, computes analytics, asks Claude for the written sections, emails the letter, commits the day's data, and rebuilds this page.
  • Market weather. Seven rules (trend vs the 200-day average, VIX and its term structure, breadth, chip-stock momentum, credit spreads, the yield curve, safe-haven demand) score 0, 1 or 2; the total sets Calm, Mixed or Stressed.
  • Risk. Beta, volatility, historical value at risk (5th percentile day), drawdowns and Sharpe from a year of daily returns. Fund look-through uses each fund's top ten holdings, so company and AI exposure are floors.
  • Stress tests. Each holding's actual return through four past selloffs, applied to today's weights. Funds that did not exist yet use a close stand-in.
  • Valuation. Ten-year two-stage unlevered free cash flow DCF: analyst growth for two years fading to 2.5%, FCF margin at its 3-year median, CAPM-based WACC, Gordon growth terminal value. Banks are skipped on purpose.
  • Privacy. No dollar amounts are published: the portfolio is an index, holdings are weights, and share counts live in an encrypted repository secret.
  • Not investment advice. Data from Yahoo Finance and Google News, may be delayed.
